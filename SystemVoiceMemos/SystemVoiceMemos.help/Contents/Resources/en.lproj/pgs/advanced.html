Find files and export audio

Reveal recordings in Finder, export combined audio, or export separate tracks for editing.

Show a recording in Finder

Use Finder when you want to share, inspect, or copy the saved audio file.

  • Control-click a recording in the list.
  • Choose Show in Finder.

Export audio

  1. Select a recording.
  2. Choose an export command from the File menu or recording shortcut menu.
  3. Choose whether to export combined audio or separate tracks, if both system audio and microphone audio were recorded.
  4. Choose a destination folder.

Understand saved files

  • Recordings are saved as M4A audio files.
  • Recordings are stored inside the app container for sandboxing.
  • Deleting a recording removes the database entry and the saved audio file.
Note: Time Machine can back up the container location if your Home Library is included in backups.

Privacy

  • Recordings stay on your Mac unless you share or move them.
  • The default recorder does not save screen video.
  • The legacy engine uses Apple's screen capture API for audio only.
