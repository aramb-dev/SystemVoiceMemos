Play recordings and choose tracks

Select a recording to play it in the app, or open the saved audio file in another app.

Play a recording

  • Double-click a recording in the list.
  • Select a recording and press Space.
  • Select a recording and click the Play button.

Move through a recording

  • Click the progress bar to seek.
  • Press Option+Left Arrow to skip back.
  • Press Option+Right Arrow to skip forward.

Choose which tracks to hear

If a recording includes a microphone track, use the playback mode control to choose what plays.

  • Both: Play system audio and microphone audio together.
  • System: Play only the system-audio track.
  • Mic: Play only the microphone track.

Open a recording in another app

  • Control-click a recording and choose Show in Finder.
  • Control-click a recording and choose Open in QuickTime Player.

If you cannot hear playback

  • Check your Mac output volume and selected output device.
  • Try switching the playback mode to Both.
  • Open the file in QuickTime Player to confirm the file contains audio.
