If recording or playback does not work

Most issues are caused by privacy permissions, another capture app, or an audio source that is not playing.

If permission is missing

  1. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  2. Choose the permission named in System Voice Memos.
  3. Turn on System Voice Memos.
  4. Quit and reopen System Voice Memos.
Tip: Screen Recording is needed only for the Legacy Screen Sharing Engine. Microphone is needed only when microphone recording is enabled.

If recording will not start

  • Make sure at least one source is selected in the toolbar or Recording settings.
  • Quit other screen recorders, audio routers, or apps that capture system audio.
  • If you just granted a permission, quit and reopen the app.

If a recording has no audio

  • Confirm audio was playing on your Mac while recording.
  • Check that the source you wanted was enabled.
  • Open the file in QuickTime Player to confirm whether the saved file contains audio.

If playback has no sound

  • Check your Mac volume and selected output device.
  • If the recording has separate tracks, choose Both in playback mode.
  • Try opening the recording in QuickTime Player.

If your Mac becomes slow

  • Turn off the Legacy Screen Sharing Engine if you do not need it.
  • Quit other apps that use screen capture.
  • Record fewer sources at the same time on lower-end Macs.
